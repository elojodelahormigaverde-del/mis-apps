How to use
The essentials

Install it

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Your data stays on this phone only. Export a backup now and then in Edit: deleting the app erases everything.

Your week

Set your weekly goals in Edit. Your streak counts the weeks in a row in which you meet all of them.

Mix Upper body, Lower body and Abs as you like: on the same day, they count as one strength session.

Sessions

Short session shows only the essential exercises. When you’re done, tap Finish session to add it to your week.

Running

In Sessions, open Running. You follow an 8-week plan with 3 runs a week: it starts with run/walk intervals and builds up to 5 km and more.

Tap a run to log it. The plan is already filled in: just correct it with what you really did. You can note the minutes, the distance (optional), the date, how it felt (Easy, OK, Hard) and a note. Each run counts as Cardio in your week.

What the app suggests:

  • After your 3 runs: start the next week, or repeat the same one if it felt hard.
  • After a break: 2 weeks without running → go back 1 week. 3 weeks → go back 2 weeks. 4 weeks or more → start again from week 1. You always decide.
  • After week 8: you move to My plan. It starts from what you did in week 8, and each new week suggests the same as the week before. Change it with Edit my plan.

Change week lets you jump to any week at any time. Your past runs always stay in your history.

▶ Timer tells you when to run and when to walk, with a beep and a voice. Keep the screen on and the sound up. At the end, it opens the log for you.

Logging and progress

Log the weight and reps of each set. RIR is how many more reps you could still do: stop with about 2 left in the tank.

Each time, try to do at least one more rep in total than last time. When all your sets reach the top of the rep range, the app tells you to go up in weight.

Tried a heavier weight and it didn’t work out? Log it as a test set: it won’t count.

Progress charts

In Progress, tap an exercise to see its chart. There are three views:

Estimated 1RM: the most weight you could lift once, estimated from your best set of the day. It goes up when you get stronger, even if you change the weight or the reps. Example: 3 kg × 12 reps ≈ 4.2 kg.

Volume: the total work of the session: weight × reps, added up over all your sets. It goes up when you do more reps or lift more weight.

Weakest set: the lightest weight you used that day. It goes up once all your sets have moved to a heavier weight.

Exercises without weights only show total reps. Test sets never count.

What you can edit

In Edit, with the PIN 1234:

  • Weekly goals: how many sessions per week for each category.
  • Your activities: add your own (yoga, walk…) to any category.
  • Exercises: name, rep range, RIR, tempo, technique notes, video, image and load levels.
  • Essential exercises: the ones included in the short session, for each session.
  • New exercises: add them to any session.
  • Remove exercises you don’t want. They move to Deleted exercises (in Sessions and Progress), keep their history, don’t count as a session, and can be restored at any time.
  • Backups: export your data, or restore it from a file.

Without a PIN:

  • Text size: Normal, Large or Extra large, at the top of Edit.
  • Order of the sessions: in Sessions, tap ↕ Reorder and use the arrows.
  • Order of the exercises: open a session, tap ↕ Reorder and use the arrows.
  • Running: Change week and Edit my plan, in the Running screen.
